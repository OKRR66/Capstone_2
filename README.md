# EmporiUm Sales Territory Analysis
### Capstone 2 — Year Up United Data Analyst Training Academy

## Project Overview
This project analyzes in-store sales performance for two territories in the Northeast region 
of EmporiUm, a student bookstore chain that sells tech gear, art supplies, and other products. 
The primary territory analyzed is **Erbayne Middleton's Maine territory** (6 stores), 
compared against **Shruti Reddy's Maryland territory** (9 stores).

## Territories Analyzed
| Territory | Manager | State | Stores |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Erbayne Middleton | Maine | 818, 819, 820, 821, 822, 823 |
| Comparison | Shruti Reddy | Maryland | 731, 732, 733, 734, 735, 736, 737, 738, 739 |

## Data
- **StoreSales** — Transaction records (2022–2025)
- **StoreDetail** — Store locations and territory managers
- **Products** — Product names and category IDs
- **ProductCategories** — Category and subcategory names
- **customer_list** — Rewards program customer information

## Analysis Summary
- Monthly and yearly revenue trends by territory
- Store performance rankings
- Top customers by total spend
- Product category analysis and growth trends
- Marketing recommendations for next quarter

## Key Findings
- Maine grew **149%** from 2022 to 2025, compared to Maryland's **41%**
- Maine's revenue per capita ($15.94) is higher than Maryland's ($9.86)
- **Technology & Accessories** is the top revenue category in both territories
- **Textbooks** show a slight downward trend driven by digitalization
- North Harford (Store 736) is a significant outlier with ~1,450 transactions/month
